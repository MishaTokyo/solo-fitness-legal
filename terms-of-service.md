## 4. Premium, diamonds, and the shop

The paid product in Solo Fitness is **Solo Fitness Premium**, one auto-renewing
yearly subscription. You must be signed in to subscribe. Core tracking
(workouts, nutrition, and basic friends features) stays available on the free
plan.

### 4.1 What Premium includes

While the subscription is active, Premium includes the benefits shown in the
app at the time of purchase. Today those are:

- a higher daily allowance for AI meal photos and AI meal logs;
- no daily cap on workouts;
- premium courses for as long as the subscription is active;
- group chats and co-workouts without passes, up to 5 people;
- a Premium status badge next to your nickname, visible to friends;
- exclusive companion armor. Armor unlocked with Premium stays on your account
  after the subscription ends.

When Premium ends, daily limits and pass-free social access return to the free
plan, and the Premium badge is removed. Courses that were only included with
Premium close, but your progress in them is kept. A course you bought
separately with diamonds stays. Armor already unlocked stays.

Free daily allowances, and anything still priced in diamonds, are shown in the
app and can change in later versions.

Payment is processed by **Google Play** (Android) or the **Apple App Store**

Premium renews automatically once a year at the then-current store price unless

You can manage or cancel at any time in App Store settings, or in Google Play →

Uninstalling the app, or deleting your Solo Fitness account, does **not**

Diamonds are a reward currency inside the app. You earn them by training — for
example daily quests, streaks, levels, and bosses — and you spend them in the
shop. **Diamonds cannot be bought with money** in the current app. They have no

The shop can offer items paid with diamonds, such as extra AI credits,
co-workout passes, a nickname change, themes, and buying a course so it stays
yours after Premium ends. The catalog and diamond prices can change. Earlier
versions of the app sold consumable diamond packs. Those packs are no longer
