Delete your account

Updated 19 June 2026

You can permanently delete your Solo Fitness account at any time. This page exists to satisfy Google Play's requirement that account deletion is possible without installing the app.

Option A — Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Solo Fitness on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm Delete forever in the dialog.

The app will sign you out and return you to the welcome screen. Your account row is removed from our database and the deletion cascades to every table that holds your data.

Option B — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by writing from the email address associated with your account to:

mikesot7@gmail.com

Please include the wording “I request deletion of my Solo Fitness account”. We will process the request within 14 days and reply when it is done. We will only act on requests sent from the email address that owns the account.

What gets deleted

Both options delete everything we store about you:

  • your authentication record (email, password hash, OAuth link);
  • your profile (nickname, body weight, level, rank, XP);
  • every workout you logged and every workout template;
  • every meal, nutrition goal, and custom food entry;
  • your body-weight history and meal-slot configuration;
  • your friendships (both directions);
  • locally cached data on your device.

What we keep

None of your personal data is retained. We may keep aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example: total number of workouts ever logged across all users) that cannot be linked back to you. Backups are overwritten within 30 days of deletion.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. You will need to sign up again from scratch if you want to use Solo Fitness later.
